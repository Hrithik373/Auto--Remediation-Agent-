## ITBench-Lite (IBM Research) — Apache License 2.0

`fixtures/incidents/itbench-sre-scenario-*.json` are **derived from** the SRE scenarios in
[`ibm-research/ITBench-Lite`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ibm-research/ITBench-Lite), licensed under
the [Apache License, Version 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

**Modifications.** These files are not copies of the dataset. `scripts/convert_itbench.py` samples and
compacts the raw snapshot telemetry (logs, traces, k8s events, span metrics) to a few tens of KB per
scenario, reshapes alerts and topology into this project's fixture schema, and adds converter-authored
oracle labels. Every label records its origin in a `provenance` field: `itbench_derived` (mapped from the
scenario's ground-truth `recommended_actions`), `converter_synthesized` (generated by this project, not
benchmark ground truth), or `human_reviewed`. No raw ITBench files are redistributed in this repository.
